# PIN SHOT: Gemini prompts for angles, opened shots and group photos

Single-bottle prompts: upload one flavor image from `assets/` and replace [FLAVOR] with its name (for example VSL or WHISKEY COLA).
Group prompts: upload all eight flavor images (not the template).

Every prompt starts with the same lock line so the bottle doesn't drift:

> Keep the bottle design identical to the uploaded image: same bottle shape, glass, grip ribs, label text and layout, fruit artwork, liquid colors, smoked grey cap, ribbed window, lid, small hinge on the left, black latch tab on the right, and the copper-orange ring on a short straight stem.

## Tips

- One flavor per request for single-bottle shots. Gemini keeps a single bottle consistent much better than eight.
- Group shots with eight bottles may garble small text. If they do, generate two groups of four and combine them, or accept slightly soft small print while checking that the flavor names stay correct.
- Keep people out, or show only adult hands. Avoid college settings, drinking games or anything suggesting fast drinking. Alcohol marketing codes flag those, and they would undercut the brand.
- Suggested file names: `pin-shot-<flavor>-<shot>.jpeg`, for example `pin-shot-paloma-open.jpeg`, `pin-shot-group-lineup.jpeg`.
