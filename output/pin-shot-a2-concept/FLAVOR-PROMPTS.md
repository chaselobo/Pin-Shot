# PIN SHOT flavor prompts

Upload `template-bottle.jpg` with one prompt at a time. Every prompt fills in the same four things: flavor name, side artwork, bottle liquid and the spirit in the cap.

## VSL

```text
Using this exact template image, make the VSL flavor. Change only these four things:

1. Flavor name: replace "FLAVOR NAME" with "VSL" in the same bold black condensed font, same letter height, weight and color, in the same position under "PIN SHOT".
2. Label artwork: add photorealistic fresh lime slices with bright green splashes and droplets, wrapping around both side edges of the bottle. On the left, one large piece at mid-height, partly cut off by the bottle's edge. On the right, two pieces stacked, one at mid-height and one lower down. Splashes and droplets trail from them up toward the shoulder and down toward the base. The artwork stays at the sides and never covers the text.
3. Bottle liquid: fill the bottle with a pale, slightly cloudy lime-green soda with fine rising bubbles, up to where the shoulder starts curving in toward the neck, with a clearly visible flat liquid line and a small air gap above it.
4. Shot cup: fill the clear inner cup inside the cap about three-quarters full with clear vodka, with a visible surface line, a slight meniscus and a soft highlight, and a small air gap under the lid.

Keep everything else exactly the same: bottle shape, glass, grip ribs, "PULL · SWIRL · SIP", "PIN SHOT", "ALCOHOLIC COCKTAIL · 21+", the smoked grey cap, ribbed window, lid, hinge, black latch tab, the copper-orange ring and short stem, lighting, white background, camera angle and framing. The two liquids stay separate. Fruit colors appear only on the label artwork and the bottle liquid.
```

## Fixes if a result drifts

- Label restyled: add "The only text that changes is the flavor name line."
- Name the wrong size: add "The flavor name is the same height as 'FLAVOR NAME' in the template, on one line."
- Artwork covers the text: add "Keep a clear gap between the artwork and the lettering."
- Cap or ring picks up a fruit color: add "The cap stays smoked grey and the ring stays copper-orange."
- A clear spirit picks up the mixer's color: add "The spirit in the cap stays perfectly clear and colorless."
- Liquid not visible: add "The liquid's surface line is a bright horizontal highlight just below the shoulder."
- Whiskey Cola text hard to read: add "The label area behind the text is a frosted white panel."
