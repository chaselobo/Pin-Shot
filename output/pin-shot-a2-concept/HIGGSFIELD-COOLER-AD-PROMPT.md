# PIN SHOT: Higgsfield "cooler" video ad (20 seconds)

A loose prompt for Higgsfield. It sets the story, the bottle and the pin, and leaves camera, cuts and pacing to the generator. Set the length to 20 seconds and make a few versions.

**Upload to Higgsfield:**
- The multi-angle PIN SHOT VSL product sheet (the bottle reference)
- The house party room scene
- Character 1 photo (the one who grabs the first bottle)
- Character 2 photo (the friend who leads the rush to the cooler)

**Changes from the original brief** (brand rules in `CLAUDE.md`, plus standard alcohol-ad codes that Meta, TikTok and YouTube enforce):
- **Not a college party.** The room stays, but everyone reads as late twenties and up. If the uploaded scene shows young-looking people, college shirts, pennants, red cups or beer pong, use a version without them or those details will leak into the video.
- **Chugging is in, by request.** Everyone tips their head back and chugs at the end. Heads-up: this goes against the "no fast-drinking cues" rule in `CLAUDE.md`, and Meta, TikTok and YouTube usually reject paid alcohol ads that show chugging. It should be fine for a concept video or organic posts. For paid ads, make a second version where they clink and drink instead.
- **"PIN SHOT" is two words**, the way it's printed on the bottle.
- **End-card text:** video models often misspell words. If the "PIN SHOT / PULL, MIX, SHOOT" card comes out garbled, cut it off and add the text in CapCut or Canva, with "21+ only. Please drink responsibly." underneath.

**Fixes after the first Higgsfield run:**
- The pull invented a second pin instead of pulling the ring that's on the bottle. The prompt now says the ring already sticking out of the cap is the pin, it's the only pin, and the same ring is what comes out.
- The lid came off with the shot cup still attached. Now only the small top lid flips back on its hinge. The shot cup and the rest of the cap stay on the bottle, and the shot cuts away as soon as the lid pops.
- In the group shot, people drank without pulling their pins. The group pull is now its own shot: everyone holds up sealed bottles with rings showing, all rip their pins together and raise them, the lids pop, and only then do they drink.
- The shot cup still came up with the lid. The prompt now describes the lid as an empty flip-top dome and the shot cup as a fixed part of the cap that never moves.
- Added a slow-motion zoom on the vodka pouring into the drink right after Character 1 pulls the pin.
- Character 1 picked the bottle up by the ring. A new HOLDING THE BOTTLE block says everyone holds the bottle body, and the ring is only touched by the other hand at the pull.
- The whole cap came off the bottle with the pull. A new THE CAP NEVER COMES OFF block locks the cap onto the bottle, and the pull is now described as a sideways yank, not upward.
- Kept from the runs so far: the slow-motion pin pull. The prompt now asks for it outright, and the friends watch Character 1's pull first, then get hyped and rush the cooler to try it themselves.
- The last drinking shot was a sip. It's now a real chug for about two seconds, then a hard cut to the end card.

## Prompt

```text
A realistic, high-energy drinks commercial for PIN SHOT, a single-serve cocktail with a vodka shot sealed inside its cap. Loud, upbeat party music.

PRODUCT: Match the uploaded PIN SHOT product sheet exactly in every shot: a clear, squat bottle with grip ribs at the base and a pale lime-green drink; a label with huge black stacked "PIN SHOT" and "VSL" underneath; a smoked grey cap with a small rounded lid on top, a ribbed see-through window showing a clear shot cup of vodka inside, and a dark band at the bottom; a black latch tab down the right side of the cap; and one copper-orange finger ring sticking out to the right from the bottom of that latch tab. The bottle is about the size of a smartphone and fits in one hand.

THE PIN: The copper-orange ring already on the bottle is the pin. It is the only pin. Its long, straight copper-orange stem is hidden inside the cap. To open, a finger hooks through that exact ring and yanks it sideways, straight out to the right, level with the cap, never upward; the long stem slides out of the side of the cap after it, and the whole pin comes free in one piece. No new or extra pin ever appears. When the pin is out, a small hole opens in the bottom of the shot cup and the clear vodka pours down through it in a glassy stream into the lime drink below, with a swirl of bubbles. Then the lid snaps up and flips back on its hinge on the left side.

THE LID AND THE SHOT CUP: The lid is a thin, shallow, empty dome, like the flip-top lid on a water bottle. Nothing is attached underneath it. The clear shot cup is a fixed part of the cap body, built in below the lid like a funnel; it never moves, never lifts out and never comes up with the lid. When the lid flips open, the empty clear shot cup is still sitting down inside the cap, and the ribbed window, the dark band and the latch tab are all still on the bottle. Nothing bursts or breaks. The drink comes out through the open top of the cap.

THE CAP NEVER COMES OFF: The whole cap is permanently locked onto the bottle. It never lifts, unscrews, pops off or separates from the bottle, before, during or after the pull, and the bottle's neck and threads are never visible. The pull only moves the pin out sideways; the cap and bottle stay together as one piece the whole time. The vodka pours inside the sealed cap and neck, never through open air.

HOLDING THE BOTTLE: Everyone always holds the bottle by its body, the whole hand wrapped around the clear ribbed bottle below the cap, like holding a soda bottle. Nobody ever lifts, carries or holds the bottle by the ring, the cap or the latch tab. The ring is only touched at the moment of the pull, by the other hand's index finger, while the first hand keeps holding the bottle body.

SETTING AND PEOPLE: The uploaded house party room at night, full of friends. Everyone is a grown adult in their late twenties or older. Character 1 and Character 2 are the people in the uploaded photos; keep their faces recognizable.

STORY: Character 1 reaches into an ice-filled cooler, wraps his hand around the body of a PIN SHOT and pulls it out, ice water dripping off it. His friends, Character 2 among them, gather around him and watch, curious, all eyes on the bottle. Close on the cap: holding the bottle body in one hand, he hooks the index finger of his other hand through the copper-orange ring and rips the pin out sideways to the right, hard and fast, his hand moving out to the side, not up. Show the pull in dramatic slow motion, the hero shot of the ad: the ring and long stem sliding out of the side of the cap until the pin comes free in his hand, while the cap stays firmly on the bottle. Then zoom in tight on the bottle: an extreme close-up, in slow motion, of the clear vodka pouring down out of the bottom of the shot cup in a glassy stream into the pale lime drink, bubbles swirling up, seen through the clear plastic, with the cap still sitting on top of the bottle. Then the lid snaps open, with the shot cup staying put inside the cap. Cut right as the lid pops. Next shot: back to normal speed, the watching friends erupt, cheering and grabbing each other, hyped to try it themselves. He raises the bottle and drinks while they, led by Character 2, rush the cooler and grab their own PIN SHOTs by the bottle body, each one still sealed with its copper-orange ring on. Group shot: they gather shoulder to shoulder and hold their sealed bottles up together, every ring clearly visible. Every person holds their bottle by the body in one hand and hooks a finger of the other hand through their own ring and they all rip their pins out sideways at the same moment, every cap staying on its bottle, each one pulling the ring already on their bottle. The pulled pins go up in the air in their fists, the shots drop, and every lid snaps open down the line. Nobody drinks until their pin is out and their lid is open. Then they clink bottles, tip their heads all the way back with the bottles upside down, and chug hard for about two seconds, a real chug, not a sip. Hard cut straight from the chug to the ending.

ENDING: A clean dark end card with "PIN SHOT" in big bold letters and "PULL, MIX, SHOOT" underneath.

You choose the camera angles and cuts. Make it feel like a real, polished drinks ad.
```

## If something goes wrong

- **It still invents a pin:** add "Keep the camera on the ring that is already on the bottle the whole time the finger hooks and pulls it." If that doesn't work, start the clip already close on the cap, with the finger through the ring.
- **The lid still takes the shot cup with it:** add "The open bottle looks exactly like the open view on the product sheet: lid flipped back on the left, clear shot cup still sitting in the cap." Uploading the open view on its own, as an extra reference, helps the most.
- **Someone holds it by the ring again:** add "His hand is wrapped around the bottle body; the ring hangs free until the other hand pulls it."
- **The cap still comes off:** add "Only the orange pin moves sideways; the grey cap does not move at all." Uploading the front view on its own also helps, because it shows the ring pointing out to the side.
- **The pour doesn't show up:** add "The camera holds on the vodka pouring into the drink before the lid opens."
- **Bottle drifts** (label changes, ring turns gold, the cap looks different): add "The bottle never changes; it matches the uploaded product sheet exactly."
- **The crowd looks young:** add "everyone visibly in their late twenties and thirties".
- **They sip instead of chugging:** add "heads tipped all the way back, bottles upside down, chugging the whole thing in one go".
- **The group still drinks without pulling:** add "Every bottle in the group starts sealed with its orange ring on, and every ring gets pulled out on camera before anyone drinks."
- **Too much happens and it feels rushed:** cut the line about Character 1 drinking before the friends notice, so there's more room for the group pull.
