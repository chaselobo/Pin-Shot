# PIN SHOT: Concept A2, Flap-Back Gate

## Approved mockup

`approved-vodka-lime-mockup-v2.jpg` is the current approved exterior for the Vodka Lime flavor (color-corrected by the user, 2026-10-01): white background, copper-orange ring and stem. `approved-vodka-lime-mockup.jpg` is the earlier grey-background version. Use it as the appearance reference for future edits and the other flavors.

What the approved image establishes:

- Clear, squat glass-look bottle with grip ribs at the base, PIN SHOT label, lime splash artwork, "PULL · SWIRL · SIP" on the shoulder.
- Smoked translucent cap: rounded lid on top, ribbed window showing an empty clear shot cup, dark lower band.
- Small hinge on the left of the lid.
- Black latch tab running down the right side of the cap from the lid to below the dark lower band.
- Short, straight, horizontal orange stem coming out of the foot of the latch tab, attached at the left edge of an open orange finger ring.
- No orange visible inside the cap. The gate paddle sits behind the dark lower band.

## Edit chain that produced the approved image

Each step was an image edit of the previous result, changing one thing at a time.

1. **Pin enters the cap.** Removed the orange rod lying across the front of the lower band. The pin enters the right side of the cap through the latch tab.
2. **No orange inside the cap.** Removed the orange rod and T-piece visible through the translucent window. Only the ring and stem outside stay orange.
3. **Latch like a fire extinguisher pin.** Extended the black latch tab down past the dark lower band with a rounded foot. Made the stem short, straight and horizontal, coming out of the foot. Removed the vertical, bent orange section.
4. **Open ring.** Stopped the stem at the ring's left edge so the ring's center is an empty hole.

Prompts that held the rest of the image steady always ended with an explicit list of what must not change (bottle, label, lime artwork, cap, lid, hinge, colors, lighting, background, framing), plus the rule that the only orange in the image is the ring and the short stem.

## Status

Appearance approved. The mechanism is still unvalidated: seals, flap recovery after storage, pull force (especially with carbonated mixers) and shelf life need the bench tests listed in `a2-concept.html`.
