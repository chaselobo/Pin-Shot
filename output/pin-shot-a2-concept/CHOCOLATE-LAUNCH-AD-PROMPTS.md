# PIN SHOT: Chocolate launch ad (5 scenes + end card)

Six clips to make one at a time and stitch together, about 28 seconds total. Each prompt stands alone, so paste the whole block every time.

**Before you start:**
- Pick **one aspect ratio** (9:16 for TikTok/Reels) and use it for every clip, or they won't line up when you stitch them.
- Upload the chocolate bottle image with every scene. For the end card, upload `assets/logo/pin-shot-logo-white.png` (the white logo, made for dark backgrounds) and the glowing pin image (`assets/ChatGPT Image Oct 1, 2026, 10_08_35 PM.png`).
- These prompts assume a creamy milk-chocolate drink with a clear shot in the cap, in the same bottle and cap as VSL. If your chocolate render looks different, the "match the uploaded image" line wins.

**Scene order:**

| # | Scene | Length | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Tease | ~4 s | Chocolate swirls away to reveal the bottle |
| 2 | The Craving | ~5 s | Slow-motion dessert-commercial macro shots |
| 3 | The Pull | ~5 s | Hero slow-motion pin pull and shot drop |
| 4 | After Dinner | ~6 s | Friends swap dessert for PIN SHOTs |
| 5 | New in the Lineup | ~4 s | Chocolate takes center stage in the flavor lineup |
| 6 | End Card | ~4 s | Logo, HIGH PROTEIN DESSERT, JOIN THE WAITLIST |

## 3. The Pull (hero shot)

```text
PRODUCT: Match the uploaded chocolate PIN SHOT bottle exactly: a clear, squat bottle about the size of a smartphone with grip ribs around the base, full of a rich, creamy, opaque milk-chocolate drink; the label from the uploaded image with huge black stacked "PIN SHOT"; a smoked grey cap with a small rounded flip lid on top, a ribbed see-through window showing a clear shot cup of clear spirit inside, and a dark band at the bottom; a black latch tab down the right side of the cap; and one copper-orange finger ring sticking out to the right from the bottom of that tab. SCENE: Tight close-up on the cap. One adult hand is wrapped around the bottle body; the index finger of the other hand hooks through the copper-orange ring and rips it out sideways to the right, hard and fast. Show the pull in dramatic slow motion: the long copper-orange stem sliding out of the side of the cap until the pin comes free in the fist, while the cap stays firmly on the bottle. Through the ribbed window, the clear shot pours down out of the bottom of the shot cup in a glassy stream, and where it hits the chocolate below, the surface swirls and marbles. Then the lid snaps up and flips back on its hinge on the left, the empty shot cup still sitting inside the cap. Cut right as the lid pops, on a hard beat drop.
```

## If something goes wrong

- **It invents a second pin or pulls upward** (scene 3 and 4): add "Keep the camera on the ring already on the bottle the whole time the finger hooks and pulls it sideways."
- **The cap comes off:** add "Only the orange pin moves; the grey cap does not move at all."
- **The drink turns see-through or watery:** add "thick, creamy, opaque milk chocolate, like a chocolate milkshake".
- **The lineup scene garbles the other bottles:** skip the lineup and replace scene 5 with the chocolate bottle slowly turning on a glowing black pedestal.
- **The crowd looks young:** add "everyone visibly in their late twenties and thirties".

## Notes

- **No chugging in this one.** It's a dessert, so everyone sips, which also keeps it inside the 21+ rules in `CLAUDE.md` and makes it usable for paid ads on Meta, TikTok and YouTube.
- **"High protein" on an alcoholic drink:** fine for a concept or waitlist video. Before running it as a paid ad or putting it on a real label, check it: US alcohol rules (TTB) limit health and nutrition claims and need the protein amount listed, and ad platforms can flag it.
- Say "join the waitlist", not "buy now". The bottle is a render of a product still in development.
