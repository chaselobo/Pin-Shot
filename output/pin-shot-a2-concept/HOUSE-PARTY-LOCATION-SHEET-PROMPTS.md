# PIN SHOT: House party location sheet prompts

A location sheet for the "night starts here" scene: a group of friends in a lived-in shared house, crowded into the living room, all raising a PIN SHOT together before heading out.

**Brand note:** the brief asked for a college pregame at a senior house. Per the brand rules (`CLAUDE.md`) and standard alcohol-ad codes, it's written as a **shared rental house with friends in their late twenties and thirties**: no college signs, no drinking games, no chugging. The energy is the same: a packed room, everyone in a line, about to drink together.

Upload **`approved-vsl-filled-mockup.jpg`** (this folder) with prompts 2 and 3 so the bottle doesn't drift. Prompt 1 is the empty room, so it doesn't need the bottle.

Paste the **ROOM** block into every prompt exactly as written. It's what keeps every angle looking like the same room.

## 1. Location sheet: empty room, six angles

```text
ROOM: The living room of an older, lived-in two-story rental house shared by a group of adult friends. Worn honey-colored hardwood floors with a faded red-and-cream Persian rug. Off-white plaster walls with crown molding. Back wall: a big three-panel bay window with sheer curtains, night outside, string lights draped across the top. Left wall: a brick fireplace painted white, mantel crowded with candles, a vinyl record player and mismatched framed photos and gig posters. Right wall: a wide open archway into a small kitchen with a white tile backsplash, a kitchen island and pendant lights. Furniture: a big slouchy olive-green velvet sectional along the left side, a scratched wooden coffee table, a beanbag, a tall leafy fiddle-leaf fig plant by the window, a floor lamp with a fabric shade. A copper-orange neon sign on the wall above the sectional that reads only "CHEERS" in script. Lighting: warm and low; the string lights, the floor lamp, candles on the mantel and a lime-green LED strip glowing along the top of the kitchen cabinets. Evening, cozy, slightly messy in a lived-in way: jackets on the couch arm, sneakers by the door, a speaker on the bookshelf.

LOCATION SHEET: A professional film location reference sheet showing this exact same room from six different camera angles, arranged in a clean 3 x 2 grid on a dark charcoal page with thin white borders. Every panel shows the same room with identical furniture, colors, decor, lighting and layout; only the camera position changes. The room is empty of people.
Panel 1: wide establishing shot from the front doorway, seeing the whole room with the bay window at the back.
Panel 2: reverse angle from the bay window looking back toward the front door and the archway.
Panel 3: from the kitchen archway looking across the room toward the fireplace and sectional.
Panel 4: low angle from the floor near the coffee table looking up at the neon "CHEERS" sign and string lights.
Panel 5: high overhead angle from a top corner of the room showing the floor plan and furniture layout.
Panel 6: medium shot of the kitchen island through the archway, with the pendant lights and lime-green LED strip.
Small white sans-serif labels under each panel: "1 WIDE / DOORWAY", "2 REVERSE / WINDOW", "3 FROM KITCHEN", "4 LOW / NEON", "5 OVERHEAD", "6 KITCHEN ISLAND". Photorealistic, natural interior photography, 35mm lens look, consistent warm color grade. Landscape 16:9.
```

## 2. Location sheet: the party, six angles of the same moment

```text

Keep: the clear, squat, rounded bottle with horizontal grip ribs around the base; the pale lime-green liquid with small bubbles; the label printed straight on the glass with huge black stacked "PIN SHOT", "VSL" underneath and "ALCOHOLIC COCKTAIL • 21+" at the bottom; the lime halves and green splash artwork on the edges; the smoked grey translucent cap with a rounded lid, a ribbed see-through window and a dark lower band; the black latch tab down the right side of the cap; and the copper-orange finger ring. In this scene the pins have just been pulled: every bottle's lid is flipped up and back on its left hinge, with no ring, ready to drink from the open cap.

SIZE: about 15 cm tall and 8 cm wide, about the height of a smartphone, held easily in one adult hand. The bottle is always shorter than the hand holding it.

ROOM: The living room of an older, lived-in two-story rental house shared by a group of adult friends. Worn honey-colored hardwood floors with a faded red-and-cream Persian rug. Off-white plaster walls with crown molding. Back wall: a big three-panel bay window with sheer curtains, night outside, string lights draped across the top. Left wall: a brick fireplace painted white, mantel crowded with candles, a vinyl record player and mismatched framed photos and gig posters. Right wall: a wide open archway into a small kitchen with a white tile backsplash, a kitchen island and pendant lights. Furniture: a big slouchy olive-green velvet sectional along the left side, a scratched wooden coffee table, a beanbag, a tall leafy fiddle-leaf fig plant by the window, a floor lamp with a fabric shade. PEOPLE: About fifteen friends, clearly adults in their late twenties and thirties, a diverse mix of people, dressed for a night out (going-out tops, denim jackets, a blazer, sneakers and boots). The same fifteen people in the same clothes and positions appear in every panel.

THE MOMENT: Before heading out, the group has squeezed into one long, loose, curving line across the middle of the living room, shoulder to shoulder and arms around each other, from the fireplace to the kitchen archway. Everyone holds their own open PIN SHOT VSL bottle raised up at chest-to-face height, all at the same moment, about to take a drink together. Photorealistic, candid party photography, 35mm lens look, warm tungsten light with lime-green and copper-orange accents, consistent color grade across all panels. Landscape 16:9.
```

## Notes

- **Consistency:** image tools drift between panels. If a panel changes the room, re-run and add: "Same room as the other panels: same sectional, same rug, same neon sign, same bay window."
- **Bottle drift:** if labels change or the ring shows up on an opened bottle, add: "Every bottle matches the uploaded image exactly, except the lid is flipped open and the pin is gone."
- **Too many people:** if fifteen comes out muddy, drop to ten.
- **Keep it 21+:** if the tool makes the crowd look young, add "everyone visibly in their late twenties and thirties". No college shirts, pennants, red cups or beer pong tables.
- Show "about to drink together" as a raised-bottle cheers, not chugging or racing.
