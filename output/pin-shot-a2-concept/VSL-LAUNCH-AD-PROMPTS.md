# PIN SHOT: VSL launch ad prompts

Upload **`approved-vsl-filled-mockup.jpg`** (this folder) with every prompt. It is the approved bottle. Don't use other images as the reference.

Catchphrase: **PULL, MIX, SHOOT!** Each word lands on its action:
- **PULL!** A finger rips the ring out, the shot drops and the lid pops. The beat drops when the pin comes free.
- **MIX!** A quick two-second swirl.
- **SHOOT!** Bottles clink and everyone drinks from the open cap.

Every prompt starts with the same three blocks so the image tool can't drift:
1. **BOTTLE**: the approved image, described piece by piece.
2. **SIZE**: real size from the concept sheet (about 15 cm tall, 8 cm wide, 355 mL).
3. **PIN**: how the A2 Flap-Back Gate pin actually works (`a2-concept.html`, `latch-reference.png`).

The cap never explodes or bursts. The excitement comes from how fast the ring gets yanked, the music, and the vodka pouring down.

## Notes

- **Order of events (from the concept):** the shot starts dropping right after the pull starts, at about 4 mm. The lid only pops at the very end, at about 50 mm, once the paddle is fully out. The pin always comes out as one piece: ring, stem and paddle.
- **Size:** the concept sheet has the bottle at about 15 cm tall and 8 cm wide, holding 355 mL. It is single-serve and easy to hold in one hand, but bigger than a shot glass. The 44 mL shot rides in the cap.
- **Label vs. catchphrase:** the bottle label still says PULL · SWIRL · SIP, and the prompts keep it unchanged. PULL, MIX, SHOOT! lives in the ad's captions and chant.
- "SHOOT!" is shown as a cheers and a drink, not chugging or racing, and the crowd is clearly grown-up (late twenties and up). No college settings, no drinking games.
- Say "coming soon" or "join the waitlist", not "buy now". These are renders of a product still in development.
- The ring is always copper-orange (`#d0703a`). If the tool makes it gold or red, re-run with "copper-orange, like a new penny".
- If the bottle drifts (label changes, the ring moves, orange shows up inside the cap), re-run and add: "The only things that move are the hand and the pin. Everything else matches the uploaded image exactly."
