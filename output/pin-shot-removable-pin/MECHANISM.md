# PIN SHOT: removable pull-pin mechanism proposal

## Scope and status

Preserve the approved squat PET bottle, bright printed sleeve, black molded flip lid and orange ring. The user's new requirement is that one pull releases the liquor and leaves the ring and complete pin fully detached. The lid stays attached.

This is a concept architecture with a visual explanation, not manufacturing CAD, a cost quote, a tested prototype, or proof of complete mixing. The image simplifies geometry and does not resolve all sealing, venting and air-return paths. Its illustrated ramp, follower and film shapes should not be copied directly into tooling.

## Recommended architecture

1. **Removable ring-pin:** One molded plastic ring and short flat slider with an underside cam ramp. A dry external guide carries the slider. No tether connects the ring-pin to the bottle.
2. **Captive follower:** A molded press piece constrained to move down as the pin is withdrawn. Ramp geometry must produce increasing downward displacement during withdrawal. A stop and retaining feature keep it inside the closure after the pin exits.
3. **Flexible sealed barrier:** A diaphragm between the dry guide and the liquor reservoir transfers movement without a sliding shaft penetrating the liquid boundary. The pin's exit must never communicate with either drink.
4. **Captive membrane opener:** An open-frame piece below the diaphragm opens a controlled region of the reservoir's bottom film. The design target is one retained flap, with no detached seal fragments or loose opening parts.
5. **Liquor reservoir:** A small sealed cup beneath the cap, with a large outlet and a separate air-return path so drainage does not depend on liquid and air fighting through a tiny hole. A possible molded return passage terminates in reservoir headspace and is opened with the outlet. Passage dimensions and final liquid fill heights remain unresolved.
6. **Retained drinking lid:** Its latch holds until the seal-opening stroke is complete. A molded flexure supplies the intended snap motion. A clear drinking passage bypasses the reservoir.

## Intended sequence

| Phase | Required behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Stored | Both drinks isolated; pin retained against accidental movement; lid secured. |
| Initial pull | Tamper feature gives way. For carbonated variants, begin controlled venting while the lid remains restrained. |
| Actuation | Pin ramp drives follower down through diaphragm; captive opener opens reservoir film and air-return path. Liquor release begins. |
| Final travel | Opener has completed its stroke; lid latch releases after pressure conditions permit opening; pin exits its guide completely. |
| Open | Ring-pin is a separate dry piece in the user's hand. Lid, follower, opener and seal remain attached to the package. Drainage may continue after pin extraction. |

A short travel interval alone is not proof of sufficient pressure release, particularly during a fast yank. Carbonated operation needs validated venting and lid restraint. Do not use beverage pressure to launch the lid.

## Cost and assembly intent

Use the same bottle and closure across flavors. Aim for a molded ring-pin, molded housing/lid, a reservoir/diaphragm assembly, one captive opener/follower assembly and a heat-sealed bottom film. Whether these can be consolidated, their material compatibility, tooling cost and filling-line implications require supplier review. No low unit price is established by the rendering.

Install/arm the ramp-pin and follower in an assembly order that cannot open the reservoir prematurely. Validate retention, creep and sealing after storage. Shape the ring with rounded edges and enough section to tolerate a brisk pull without breaking into fragments.

## First functional prototype checks

- Show that a complete pull always opens the reservoir before the pin becomes free.
- Show that a partial pull does not release the drinking lid prematurely.
- Verify that the pin comes out as one dry piece and leaves no fluid exit through its guide.
- Confirm that the opener and film flap stay attached.
- Measure pull force and displacement; verify follower retention after pin removal.
- Measure drainage, trapped liquid and mixing uniformity using water and a colored surrogate before developing pressure-rated carbonated samples.
- Verify drinking clearance around the reservoir.
- Develop pressure release, accidental-activation resistance, shelf life and drop resistance with a packaging engineer before treating this as a usable beverage package.

## Background reference

A flexible diaphragm driving a retained membrane opener is a documented dispensing-closure architecture:
https://patents.google.com/patent/US20080179331A1/en

That reference supports the component principle. The removable ramp pin, staged lid release and complete PIN SHOT assembly proposed here are unvalidated. No patent clearance assessment is included.
