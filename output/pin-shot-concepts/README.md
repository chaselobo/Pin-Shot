# PIN SHOT — first container concepts

Created with the built-in image generation tool. These are appearance studies and illustrative cutaways, not validated engineering drawings. Liquid colors distinguish compartments; they do not specify recipes. Pressure venting, complete drainage, uniform mixing, pull force, retained parts, food-contact materials, shelf life and filling process need development and testing. Rendered linkage details are illustrative and should not be copied directly into CAD.

## Concepts

1. **Capsule:** Rounded ribbed bottle; pin moves a linkage opening the hinged floor of a small upper spirit reservoir and releasing a captive drinking cover. Strong brand silhouette; custom cap and reservoir add complexity.
2. **Slim can:** Familiar can; ring peels open an internal spirit pod and uncovers a drinking aperture. Compact format; hidden mixing and custom lid/filling process are drawbacks.
3. **Split flask:** Side-by-side chambers; pull retracts a flexible separating membrane into a retained cap pocket and releases the spout cover. Most directly follows the removable-wall idea; long seal, film retention and retraction travel are major engineering questions.
4. **Stack cup:** Clear stacked chambers; pin linkage rotates a captive shutter to align drain ports, then releases drinking cover. Very visible separation; moving liquid-tight gate and drainage/air-return paths require development.

## Proposed experience

One continuous pull, staged to vent, connect chambers, then uncover the drinking aperture. A captive lid provides the snap. Opening two chambers does not demonstrate complete mixing. Actual timing and agitation requirements need prototype tests, especially with carbonated mixers.
