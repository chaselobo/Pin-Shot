# Pin Shot: two-plug mechanism study

Generated and edited using the built-in image tool. This is a conceptual illustration, not CAD or evidence of working hardware.

A single ring and rigid stem lift a mouth stopper and the bottom stopper of a small internal liquor reservoir. The reservoir stays in the bottle. The outer mouth and reservoir rim need separate seals. A passage around the reservoir connects the main beverage chamber to the mouth. Pulling opens the reservoir and mouth; complete mixing is not established. A tether retains the removed assembly.

Unresolved: carbonated-pressure retention and controlled venting, pull force, tamper evidence, wet stem/dripping, drainage and air paths, drinking clearance, filling, materials and seal tolerances. This version demonstrates a pull-out action; a short pin stroke and snap-open action require further design.

## Final revision

Extended the inner reservoir walls up into the neck so its rim can seal against a separate concentric sealing surface beneath the mouth stopper. Kept an annular drinking passage around the reservoir. Applied the same taller reservoir in both bottle views.
