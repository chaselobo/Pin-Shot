# Pin Shot: affordable pull-pin packaging direction

## User requirements

Grenade-like pull-pin and snap-open interaction; affordable, durable packaging for adult college-age customers. Both liquids stay separate before activation. One action releases liquor into mixer and opens the drinking opening.

## Proposed package

A short ribbed PET bottle with printed shrink sleeve; a molded polypropylene cap and retained flip lid; a short molded plastic ring-pin; a small spirit reservoir beneath the lid with a retained peel-film bottom seal. Use identical bottle and closure geometry across flavors.

The desired pull should mechanically open the small internal seal and then release the cap latch, with a molded flexure providing the lid's snap. The render shows the desired experience, not a resolved linkage. It does not show a validated pressure vent, tamper feature or manufacturing-ready assembly. The same mold across flavors is a cost-control target, not a verified quote. No unit cost has been established.

## Main engineering questions

How the external ring actuates the internal seal without leaking; controlled venting before opening for carbonated mixers; seal peel force; molded flexure performance after shelf storage; tamper evidence and accidental activation; filling both chambers; complete mixing; drainage and a clear drinking path past the reservoir; drop resistance and pressure rating. Do not use beverage pressure to propel the lid.
